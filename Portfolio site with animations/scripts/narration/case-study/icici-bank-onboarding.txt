ICICI Bank credit cards were declining thirty percent of international transactions, not because the cards didn't work abroad, but because every new or reissued card ships with international usage switched off by default, by RBI mandate. A customer lands in Tokyo, taps their card, and gets declined with no idea why.

I led the end-to-end redesign of a unified hub called iTravel, where customers declare their travel plans and the bank automatically aligns card usage and fraud monitoring to that profile. The real problem wasn't that the toggle was hidden, it was that the bank had no way of knowing a customer was about to travel until they'd already been declined. iTravel closes that information gap before the trip, reaching customers through multiple entry points since most cardholders barely open the banking app directly.

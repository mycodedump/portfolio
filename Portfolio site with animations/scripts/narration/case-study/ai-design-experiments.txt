I wanted to know how generative AI could augment a design process without stripping away creative ownership, so I ran a six-week self-initiated study, exploring where AI genuinely sped up early-stage exploration versus where it just produced plausible-looking noise. Eight external designers joined the workshop format that came out of it.

The result was roughly three times faster first prototypes, but the more useful finding was about where the human judgment still had to sit: framing the problem, deciding what to explore, and knowing which AI-generated direction was actually worth pursuing. That workshop format is now being adapted internally.

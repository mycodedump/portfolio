FASTag is mandatory for all four-wheelers on Indian highways, and ICICI Bank commands nearly twenty nine percent of the national FASTag market. Before this project, every one of those customers relied solely on iMobile or a third-party app to manage their tag. I designed the entire FASTag experience for the web platform from scratch, no brief, no precedent, just a fixed two-week deadline and three distinct user types that needed to coexist on the same platform.

Someone checking their FASTag balance is usually driving toward a toll plaza. They need an answer in a glance, so the card leads with vehicle number, balance, and a recharge button that's always in the same place, everything else stays out of the way until it's actually needed. From the landing page's tab decision, to the card states, to a single standard recharge flow regardless of entry point, every choice traced back to that same urgency.
